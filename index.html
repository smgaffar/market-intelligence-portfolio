Portfolio Projects

Market Intelligence · Sales Analysis · Business Development

Regional Sales vs. Target Achievement (FMCG / Bulk)

Diagnosed regional performance across six sales divisions by comparing actual sales against target, in both value and volume, over three months — rebuilding region-level achievement from a multi-level territory hierarchy.

3-month value achievement by region

RegionJulAugSep (MTD)
Dhaka South91%88%38%
Chittagong66%75%30%
Khulna66%73%32%
Dhaka North66%69%18%
Sylhet56%62%24%
Rajshahi43%44%30%

Insight & action

Dhaka South was the consistent leader; Rajshahi a persistent under-performer, flagged for route-to-market and coverage review. Realized average selling price ran below target, so value achievement lagged volume — informing a pricing and SKU-mix intervention.

Product Analysis · Strategic Planning · Portfolio Prioritization

Product & Segment Performance — "Akij Essential"

Broke a 58-SKU branded FMCG portfolio into categories and two strategic segments — Bulk (Rice, Flour & Dal) vs. Consumer — to guide invest / hold / fix / exit decisions.

Insight & action

The Bulk segment clearly outperformed Consumer, driven by Rice. Tea and Dal emerged as priority problem areas, and the output was translated into a BCG-style matrix to guide portfolio decisions.
