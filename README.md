## Professional Summary

I am a **market intelligence and business development professional** focused on
converting raw market data into clear, actionable business insight. My work
spans four interlocking disciplines:

| Discipline | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Market Intelligence** | Where is the market now, and where is it heading? |
| **Business Development** | Where are the growth opportunities, and how do we capture them? |
| **Strategic Planning** | Which direction should the business take, and why? |
| **Feasibility Analysis** | Is the opportunity viable, affordable, and worth the risk? |

Together these disciplines form a single decision loop: **observe the market →
size the opportunity → set the strategy → validate the business case → execute
and measure.**

## Core Domains

### 1. Market Intelligence

Systematic collection, validation, and interpretation of market data to
understand demand, competition, customers, and channel dynamics.

**What I deliver**

- **Market Research** — structured primary research (field surveys,
  interviews, observational studies) and secondary/desk research.
- **Competitive Intelligence** — benchmarking competitors across pricing,
  product portfolio, distribution, and positioning.
- **Market Sizing** — top-down and bottom-up estimation of TAM / SAM / SOM.
- **Market & Channel Audit** — coverage, density, and performance by territory
  and route-to-market.
- **Customer Insights** — segmentation and profiling, CSAT / CSI / NPS,
  and Kano / Importance–Performance (IPA) analysis.
- **Pricing Intelligence** — net-price analysis, commission structures, and
  competitive price positioning.
- **Commodity & Trend Analysis** — raw-material monitoring, seasonality,
  and demand forecasting.

**Frameworks used**

- Porter's Five Forces · PESTLE · Competitive benchmarking
- TAM / SAM / SOM market sizing
- CSAT · CSI · NPS · Kano · IPA
- The intelligence cycle: *Plan → Collect → Process → Analyze → Disseminate*

**Typical outputs**

- Market / competitor landscape reports
- KPI dashboards (executive and operational)
- Sales and share trend analysis
- Distributor and channel scorecards

### 4. Feasibility Analysis

Stress-testing an idea before capital is committed — across market, technical,
operational, financial, and legal dimensions.

**What I deliver**

- **Market Feasibility** — demand validation, target-market sizing, and
  competitive viability.
- **Technical & Operational Feasibility** — capability, capacity, supply-chain,
  and resource readiness.
- **Financial Feasibility** — investment sizing, revenue/cost modeling, and
  return metrics.
- **Risk & Sensitivity Analysis** — key-driver and scenario stress-testing.
- **Go / No-Go Recommendation** — a clear, evidence-backed decision with
  conditions and mitigation plans.

**Frameworks used**

- Financial modeling: **NPV · IRR · Payback Period · Break-even · ROI**
- Sensitivity and scenario (Monte Carlo where appropriate) analysis
- Risk register with likelihood / impact scoring
- Stage-gate and go/no-go decision criteria

**Typical outputs**

- Feasibility studies and investment memos
- Financial models with sensitivity tables
- Business cases and capital-request documents
- Risk registers and mitigation action plans

## Methodology & Frameworks

A consolidated view of the frameworks I apply across engagements.

| Area | Frameworks & Methods |
| --- | --- |
| Environment analysis | PESTLE, Porter's Five Forces |
| Internal analysis | SWOT, VRIO, value-chain analysis |
| Portfolio & growth | BCG, GE–McKinsey, Ansoff |
| Strategy choice | Porter's Generic Strategies, Blue Ocean |
| Market sizing | TAM / SAM / SOM (top-down & bottom-up) |
| Customer insight | CSAT, CSI, NPS, Kano, IPA, segmentation |
| Financial validation | NPV, IRR, payback, break-even, ROI, sensitivity |
| Execution & tracking | Balanced Scorecard, OKRs, KPI trees, dashboards |

**Operating principles**

1. **Evidence over opinion** — every conclusion traces back to a data point or
   a documented assumption.
2. **One clean dataset** — a single validated source feeds all analysis to
   avoid inconsistency.
3. **Every KPI is a formula** — models recalculate, nothing is hardcoded.
4. **Every recommendation is actionable** — owner, deadline, and success metric.
5. **Reproducible work** — analyses are documented so they can be re-run and
   audited.

## Portfolio Projects

### Project 1 — Regional Sales vs. Target Achievement (FMCG / Bulk)

**Domain:** Market Intelligence · Sales Analysis · Business Development

**Objective:** Diagnose regional performance for a bulk FMCG distribution
business by comparing actual sales against target, both in **value** and
**volume**, across six sales divisions over three months.

**Approach**

- Consolidated **target** (per region) and **actual secondary-sales**
  (deliveries) data from an ERP warehouse.
- Mapped delivery territories to their parent regions through a multi-level
  territory hierarchy (Company → Sales Organization → Region → Area → Zone →
  Point → Section).
- Rebuilt region-level achievement in both **BDT value** and **unit volume**,
  month by month.

**Key findings (illustrative, 3-month value achievement)**

| Region | Jul | Aug | Sep (MTD) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Dhaka South | 91% | 88% | 38% |
| Chittagong | 66% | 75% | 30% |
| Khulna | 66% | 73% | 32% |
| Dhaka North | 66% | 69% | 18% |
| Sylhet | 56% | 62% | 24% |
| Rajshahi | 43% | 44% | 30% |

**Insight & action**

- **Dhaka South** was the consistent leader; **Rajshahi** was a persistent
  under-performer — flagged for route-to-market and coverage review.
- Realized average selling price ran **below** target, meaning value
  achievement lagged volume achievement — informing a pricing and SKU-mix
  intervention.

### Project 2 — Product & Segment Performance (Brand: "Akij Essential")

**Domain:** Product Analysis · Strategic Planning · Portfolio Prioritization

**Objective:** Break down a branded FMCG portfolio (58 SKUs) into categories and
segments to identify where to invest, defend, or exit.

**Approach**

- Grouped 58 SKUs into categories (Flour, Rice, Edible Oil, Salt, Lentils,
  Tea, Spices, Others) and into two strategic segments:
  **Bulk (Rice, Flour & Dal)** vs. **Consumer**.
- Compared target vs. actual sales (value and volume) per product, category,
  and segment over a three-month window.

**Key findings (illustrative, 3-month value achievement)**

| Segment / Category | Achievement |
| --- | ---: |
| **Bulk (Rice, Flour & Dal)** | **60%** |
| · Rice | 68% |
| · Flour | 59% |
| · Dal (Lentils) | 27% |
| **Consumer** | **45%** |
| · Salt | 60% |
| · Edible Oil | 50% |
| · Spices | 36% |
| · Others | 29% |
| · Tea | 11% |
| **Total portfolio** | **56%** |

**Insight & action**

- The **Bulk segment clearly outperformed Consumer**, driven by Rice (68%).
- **Tea (11%)** and **Dal (27%)** emerged as priority problem areas — Tea
  warranted a demand/positioning review; Dal a pricing and availability review.
- Portfolio output was translated into a **BCG-style matrix** to guide
  invest / hold / fix / exit decisions.

## How I Work

A repeatable, evidence-based workflow:

1. **Frame** — clarify the decision the analysis must support.
2. **Collect** — primary and secondary data into one staging area.
3. **Clean & validate** — normalize, deduplicate, and verify.
4. **Analyze** — apply the appropriate frameworks and financial models.
5. **Synthesize** — turn findings into insights and a recommendation.
6. **Communicate** — executive summary, dashboards, and an action plan with
   owners and metrics.

Every recommendation is tied back to a specific finding, and every KPI is a
formula — so the work is auditable and reproducible.
